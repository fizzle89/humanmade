What this is, plainly. A certificate records your file's SHA-256 fingerprint, your written declaration and a timestamp, sealed so any edit is detectable. It proves the file and statement have not changed since issue. It cannot prove how the work was made. Your name stands behind the declaration. Files never leave your device.

How it works

Sealed in three steps

  1. Fingerprint the file

    Your browser computes the file's SHA-256 fingerprint. Nothing uploads; the file never leaves your device.

  2. Sign your declaration

    You state, in writing and under your name, how the work was made and which tools you used.

  3. Seal and share

    The certificate seals the fingerprint, declaration and time. Anyone can re-check it on the verify page.
